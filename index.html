Why nothing repeats

Ein Sof means "without end" — the Kabbalistic name for the boundless, that which exists before form and cannot be measured or copied.

Every bracelet here borrows that idea in miniature. One arrangement of stone, wire, and colour, made a single time, then never rebuilt. When a piece leaves the workshop, its record moves to the archive — not to be reissued, not even by the person who made it.
